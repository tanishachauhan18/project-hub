How ProjectHub Works

A clear, 5-step journey from project ideation to final grade certification.

Step 1: Student Registration & Admin Verification
Step 01

Students register with their academic email and roll number. The Administrator validates credentials in the Admin Approvals queue to activate the account.

Step 2: Team Formation & Faculty Guide Request
Step 02

The team leader creates a project, invites 1 to 3 peer students (2–4 members total), defines roles, and sends a formal guide proposal to a faculty professor.

Step 3: Milestone Planning & Execution
Step 03

Teams execute project phases (SRS, Design, Implementation, Testing), upload files, commit source code, and interact with guides via the team discussion forum.

Step 4: Faculty Feedback & Milestone Sign-Off
Step 04

The faculty guide reviews uploaded SRS documents, inspects code quality, posts structured feedback and star ratings, and marks milestones complete.

Step 5: Final Submission & 5-Criteria Rubric Grading
Step 05

The student leader uploads the final report and demo links. Faculty evaluates across Presentation, Code, Docs, Viva, and Innovation (/100), producing an official grade card.
