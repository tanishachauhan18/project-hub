About The Platform

Pioneering Next-Generation Academic Project Management

ProjectHub bridges the communication gap between university students, faculty supervisors, and academic evaluation committees.

Our Core Mission

To modernize senior capstone projects, engineering dissertations, and collaborative research by offering structured versioning, rigorous milestone accountability, and transparent rubric evaluation.

Eliminate fragmented communications over WhatsApp and email
Digitize guide requests, approvals, and milestone sign-offs
Standardize 5-criteria marks grading and institutional reporting
